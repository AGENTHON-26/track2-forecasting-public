Cumulative Walk Anatomy
Track 2 · forecast_models.py · the one production model since 2026-09-27

The cumulative walk

Every card is forecast the same way: start at the last value, then take one random step per leg, one leg per horizon. The family only changes the settings. Below, the equation, what each family sets, and two real cards worked number by number, with the draws production actually writes.

valuea(hk) = lasta + Σj ≤ k tj · (L · ej)a · scalea · √(hj − hj−1)
a, k, j
asset a; horizon k; leg j is the stretch from horizon j−1 to horizon j (h0 = 0).
last
the last value on or before the as-of (0 on a log-return card).
scale
sd of one daily step × the family's widen.
ej
independent normal numbers, one per asset, fresh for every leg.
L
the Cholesky factor of the assets' step correlation: it turns independent e into correlated z = L·e.
tj
√((ν−2) ÷ W), W ~ χ²(ν), one per leg, shared by all assets in a draw: the Student-t fat tails.
The pieces

Five ingredients, each doing one job

sd of one step

sd = √( Σ (step − mean)² ÷ window )

The typical daily move over the family's window. F4 uses the EWMA sd instead, where recent steps weigh more.

scale

scale = sd × widenfamily

The family's widen (1.1 for F2, 1.2 for F1, F3, F4) makes the band a little wider than history alone.

√ of the steps

width at h = scale × √h

Independent daily steps add their variances, so h days have variance h × scale² and width scale × √h.

Cholesky L

z = L · e,  L = [[1, 0], [ρ, √(1−ρ²)]]

Makes the assets move together with their measured correlation ρ. With one asset, L = [1] and does nothing.

Student-t shock

t = √((ν − 2) ÷ W),  W ~ χ²(ν)

Usually below 1, sometimes large: rare big moves. Same variance as a normal shock, fatter tails. ν = 4 or 5.

Legs

value(h₂) = value(h₁) + leg₂

Horizon 2 is reached by walking through horizon 1, so the two horizons of a draw are linked: corr = √(h₁ ÷ h₂).

How F4 measures its sd

F4 is the only family with a halflife. Its sd weights each past step by 0.5age ÷ 63 over the last 504 rows, so the last month counts about 21% instead of 8%. Interactive walkthrough: EWMA Volatility Explorer.
